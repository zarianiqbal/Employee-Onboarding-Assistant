# Leave Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-01_

This policy explains how time off works. If anything here conflicts with local
law in your region, local law prevails.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of paid time off per year, accrued
monthly. PTO can be used for vacation or personal days. Up to **5 unused days**
carry over into the following year; anything above that is forfeited on
December 31.

To request PTO, submit the request through the onboarding dashboard at least
**two weeks** in advance for planned time off. Your manager approves requests.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 days** of paid sick leave per year, separate from PTO.
Sick leave does not carry over. For absences longer than **3 consecutive days**,
a doctor's note may be required.

## Parental Leave

New parents are eligible for **16 weeks** of paid parental leave, which must be
taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child. Notify HR at least
30 days before your intended start date where possible.

## Public Holidays

The company observes regional public holidays. The specific list depends on
your work region (US, UK, EU, APAC, or LATAM) and is published on the intranet.

## Bereavement Leave

Up to **5 days** of paid bereavement leave are available for the loss of an
immediate family member.
