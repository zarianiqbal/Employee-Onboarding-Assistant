# Company Handbook

_Last updated: 2026-01-01_

Welcome! This handbook is a high-level guide to how we work. It covers our
values, working arrangements, code of conduct, and where to get help.

## Our Values

- **Customer first** — we start from the customer's problem.
- **Own it** — we take responsibility end-to-end.
- **Default to openness** — we share context generously.
- **Grow together** — we invest in each other's development.

## Working Arrangements

We operate a **hybrid** model. Most teams are in the office two to three days a
week, with the exact schedule set by your team. Core collaboration hours are
**10:00–16:00** in your local time zone.

## Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free workplace. Treat colleagues,
customers, and partners with dignity. Report concerns to HR or through the
anonymous ethics line. Retaliation for good-faith reports is prohibited.

## Equipment & IT

Your laptop and accounts are provisioned before Day 1. Enable multi-factor
authentication immediately. Never share passwords; the company uses a secretless
security model and will never ask for your password by email.

## Benefits Overview

Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance, a retirement savings plan
with company match, paid time off, and a learning stipend. Enroll during your
first week; see the Leave Policy and Benefits pages for details.

## Getting Help

- **People / HR questions** — the People Ops team.
- **IT / equipment** — the IT service desk.
- **Anything onboarding** — ask the AI onboarding assistant in the dashboard.
