# Required Onboarding Documents

_Last updated: 2026-01-01_

To complete onboarding you must submit the following documents. Upload them
securely through the **Documents** section of the onboarding dashboard — files
go directly to encrypted storage and are only visible to HR.

## Before Day 1 (Pre-boarding)

- **Signed offer letter** — review and e-sign.
- **Government-issued photo ID** — passport or driver's license. Used to verify
  your identity and, where applicable, work eligibility.

## Day 1

- **Tax withholding form** — a W-4 in the US, or the equivalent for your region.
- **Direct deposit / banking details** — so payroll can pay you.

## Week 1

- **Emergency contact form** — at least one emergency contact.
- **Benefits enrollment selections** — your health and retirement choices.

## Notes

- Acceptable file formats: PDF, JPG, or PNG, up to 20 MB each.
- If you upload a driver's license or passport, the system may read the image to
  pre-fill your name, date of birth, and address for you to confirm.
- Your identity documents are never used by the AI assistant and are stored
  separately from company policy documents.
